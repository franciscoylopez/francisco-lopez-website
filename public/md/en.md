Process

## How I work

An end-to-end method: from the problem to the data that confirms the goal was met. Six stages that feed one another.

01

### Discovery

Interviews, data and business context to separate the real problem from the symptom before proposing a solution.

02

### UX

I turn findings into architecture and flows: what the user sees, in what order and why.

03

### Prototyping

Clickable prototypes to build shared understanding before committing development.

04

### Development

Functional definition and coordination with the technical team, from roadmap to release.

05

### Launch

Selective deployment: standard or feature flags, progressive rollout and A/B testing to assess whether the goal was met.

06

### Analytics

Instrumentation and metrics to measure real impact and feed the next discovery.
