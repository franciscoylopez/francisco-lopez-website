# Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Two things here: what I do with your data if you get in touch, and what cookies this site uses. Same approach for both: the minimum, with real names and real deadlines, and no analytics cookie without your consent.

## Your data when you write to me

This first part isn't about cookies. It's about what happens to your data if you get in touch, by email or from this site. It's the information required by article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation.

### Who the controller is

Francisco Javier López Martínez, in Valencia, Spain. There's no company behind this site: it's personal, and I write and maintain it myself. For anything to do with your data you can · [write to me](#contacto) · .

### What data I process, and what for

Whatever you give me when you write: your name, your email address and whatever you say in the message. I use it only to read you and reply. No mailing lists, no profiling, no automated decisions, no advertising.

### On what legal basis

Your own initiative. Writing to me is the step before a possible working relationship, so the legal basis is the pre-contractual measures of article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR. That's why there's no box to tick before sending: the processing starts because you decide to write.

### Who else sees it

Nobody else reads your message. The only company involved is Google, which hosts the mailbox (Gmail) and acts as processor. That means an international transfer of data to the United States, covered by the EU to US Data Privacy Framework, which Google has joined. I don't pass your data to anyone for commercial purposes.

### How long I keep it

Your message stays in the mailbox for as long as the conversation makes sense, and at most one year from the last email. After that I delete it, unless there's a legal obligation to keep it.

### What rights you have

You can ask me for access to your data, for it to be corrected or deleted, and you can also object to the processing, restrict it, or ask for it back in a portable format. Just write to the address at the end of this page. And if you think I've got it wrong, you can complain to the · [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es) · .

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. Equivalent technologies are also used, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage). Some are essential for the site to work; others, like analytics, are only used if you allow them.

## What this site uses

Here's the detail of what may load, grouped by category:

**What this site uses**

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Category |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `flm-consent` | franciscolopez.es | Stores your cookie choice so you're not asked again. | Persistent | Necessary |
| `Container (GTM)` | Google | Manages the loading of the other tags. Stores no data on its own. | — | Necessary |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes users in aggregate to measure site usage. | 2 years | Analytics |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Keeps the measurement session state. | 2 years | Analytics |
| `_clck` | Microsoft Clarity | Identifies the browser across visits to link your recorded sessions and heatmaps. | 1 year | Analytics |
| `_clsk` | Microsoft Clarity | Connects multiple page views into a single recording session. | 1 day | Analytics |

“flm-consent” is kept in the browser's local storage (localStorage); it's technically not a cookie, but it's listed here for transparency. No marketing or advertising cookies are used today.

## Legal basis

Necessary cookies load so the site can work and need no consent. Analytics cookies only load with your prior consent, which you can give, deny or withdraw at any time without affecting how the site works.

## How to manage or withdraw your consent

The first time you visit, a notice lets you accept, reject or choose by category. You can change your choice, including analytics, at any time from the button below:

Manage cookie preferences

You can also block or delete cookies from your browser settings.

## Third parties

Analytics is provided by Google (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics) and Microsoft (Clarity), which process data under their own policies. You can read them at [Google's privacy policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and at [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Embedded video

The INDYA and TheTool deep-dives embed a video hosted on YouTube. It does not load on its own: until you press play, the page shows a still image served from this domain and makes no request to Google. Once you press play, the video loads from youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube's domain that does not write advertising cookies) and Google's terms and privacy policy apply from that point on.

## Data retention

Your consent choice is stored in your browser until you change it or clear the site data. Aggregate analytics data is retained by Google according to the property settings (by default, a maximum of 14 months at user level).
